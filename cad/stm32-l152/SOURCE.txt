NUCLEO-L152RE (STM32L152RE) 3D files for Fusion 360.

Use NUCLEO-L152RE.step — this is the real MB1136 Nucleo-64 PCB (same board as NUCLEO-L152RE). Exported from ST’s official MB1136 Altium design.
